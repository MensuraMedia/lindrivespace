LinDriveSpace · scan display

Scan strip variants

The running scan moves out of the header pop-up into the top of the content area, on every page. No "entries" wording; the time is an estimated countdown (the app knows the mount's used bytes, so the bar is real progress: scanned ÷ used). Three treatments of the same information: a strip, a stats panel, a ribbon.

A · Progress strip

One line, real progress, countdown

Recommended. 56 px tall, accent top edge. Spinner, "Scanning" + path, a wide progress bar, scanned-of-used and percent, the countdown in a mono chip, pause / stop. Queue shown as a chip. Compact enough to stay visible above the tree without stealing rows.

B · Stats panel

Big percent, big countdown, queue chips

96 px tall. The percent in Ubuntu Light dominates, the countdown mirrors it on the right, the bar and a small key/value row sit between, and the queue is a row of chips (done ✓ · scanning · queued). Best when the scan itself is the thing to watch (Overview). Trade-off: costs three tree rows on the Explorer.

C · Ribbon

Slim, with a progress line on the edge

32 px. A 3 px accent line along the top edge is the progress bar; one sentence of text; queue as dots; stop only. Lowest cost in vertical space, least information. Good as the collapsed state of A when the user wants the tree.
