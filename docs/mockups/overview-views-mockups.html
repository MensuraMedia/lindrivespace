LinDriveSpace · Overview

Overview view types

The primary drive and mountpoint section gets a view switcher (segmented control, remembered in settings). Four treatments of the same mounts — this machine's nvme0n1 (/, /boot/efi, /home) and sda (/mnt/data). Hidden and virtual mounts stay in one compact group underneath in every view.

1 · Cards

Uniform cards, grouped by disk

Today's view, corrected. Every card is the same 300 px width in every group; primary/secondary badges; bar gauge; star to favourite; Scan/Rescan. Best for a handful of mounts.

2 · List

One row per mount, inline bars

Dense. Sortable columns like the Explorer: mount, device, type, used, free, total, a Share bar, last scan, actions. Scales to servers with dozens of mounts; disks are section rows.

3 · Capacity

One stacked bar per physical disk

Shows the disk, not just the mounts. Each disk is a bar; partitions are segments proportional to their size, the used part of each segment is filled in accent, unallocated space is hatched. Click a segment to scan it. Answers "where is the space on this drive" in one glance.

4 · Map

Treemap of mounts by capacity

Proportional. Every mount is a rectangle sized by its capacity; the accent fill inside each rises with usage, so a full small partition still stands out. Same treemap engine as the Analysis panel; click a rectangle to scan or open it. Best on machines with many disks of very different sizes.
